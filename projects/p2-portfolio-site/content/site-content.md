## About (for the home page)

Bright Nest Interiors is a two-person design studio in Oakland. We design rooms that
are calm, easy to clean and built around how you actually live, whether that's a
first apartment, a family kitchen or a home office you finally want to work in.

We start with a one-hour consultation in your home. You get honest advice, a clear
plan and a price before any work begins.

## Services (for the services page)

1. **Full-room design**: a complete plan for one room, including layout, colors,
   furniture and lighting. From $1,200 per room.
2. **Color consultation**: a two-hour visit to choose paint colors for up to five
   rooms, with samples. $250.
3. **Furniture sourcing**: we find and order furniture that fits your space and
   budget, at trade prices where we can. 15% of the furniture budget.
4. **Home staging**: we prepare a home for sale or rent so it photographs well and
   feels welcoming. From $900.

## Recent projects (for the home page)
